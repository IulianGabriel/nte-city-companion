Privacy

This unofficial fan companion uses your Google account identifier and email to save your activity progress, reset settings, XP and optional profile name/photo. Google sign-in only requests basic identity information. It does not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail or contacts.

Account records are stored in Cloudflare D1. They are private to your account. A secure browser cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Google access tokens are used only during sign-in and are not retained. Guest progress stays in browser storage. Clearing browser data removes guest progress unless you keep a backup.

The public activity spreadsheet belongs to Buiuga. Personal completion is never written to that sheet. Guides and fonts may load from Imgur and Google Fonts, which receive ordinary network request information. No advertising or tracking analytics are added by this app.

Use Account & sync to export your progress or sign out. Contact the maintainer to request deletion of your companion account data; this does not delete your Google account. For questions, contact the maintainer through the project’s issue tracker; don’t post private progress or account details publicly.
